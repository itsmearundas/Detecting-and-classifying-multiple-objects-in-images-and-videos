How the pipeline works

A hybrid detection-then-classification approach keeps results fast and precise.

01

YOLOv8 detects

Every frame is scanned for candidate objects — people, vehicles, animals and furniture — with bounding boxes and initial confidence.

→
02

EfficientNet‑B0 refines

Low-confidence or person-class crops are routed to a dedicated classifier for a sharper, more reliable label.

→
03

Results, annotated

You get a labeled image or video back, plus a clean summary of what was found and how confident the model is.
